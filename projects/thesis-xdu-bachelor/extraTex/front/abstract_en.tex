The choice of mechanical transmission parameters affects motion accuracy and operating stability. This example considers a simplified single degree of freedom system and relates its mass, damping, and stiffness through a dynamic model. Natural frequency and damping ratio are calculated with consistent parameter definitions and units. A comparison of stiffness values illustrates the connection between assumptions, calculations, and interpretation.

This document is an undergraduate thesis typesetting example. Its numerical results follow the stated model and do not represent equipment experiments. Figures, tables, equations, cross references, and citations demonstrate how a reproducible design or experimental study can be organized.
